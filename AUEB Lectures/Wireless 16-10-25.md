## Transmission Rate:

Aka Capacity or Linkspeed, it's measured in bits/seconds.
And it measures the rate of communicated data

## Bandwidth:

It's measured in cycles/seconds (Hertz).
It's constrained by the transmitter and the medium it travels through.
